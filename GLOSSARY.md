# Turbobard administration

Administration of Turbobard's Sound catalog and the audio files it makes available
to players. Terms shared with Turbobard retain their product meanings.

## Language

**Sound**:
An audio item available for use in an Environment, either a Loop or a One-shot.
_Avoid_: Track (legacy term)

**Loop**:
A Sound intended for continuous music or ambiance through seamless repetition.

**One-shot**:
A Sound with one or more short variations played individually at a configured
interval.
_Avoid_: Oneshoot, one shot

**Pack**:
A curated collection of Sounds with preset volumes and, where applicable,
One-shot timing, which can be added to an Environment together.

**Environment**:
A user-created collection of Sounds configured for a tabletop scene.
_Avoid_: Group

**Sound catalog**:
The searchable collection of Sounds, Packs, and their descriptive information
available to Turbobard users.

**Source audio**:
Original audio supplied as the starting point for preparing a Sound.

**Prepared audio**:
Encoded audio produced from source audio and ready for review before publication.

**Audio replacement**:
Prepared audio that supersedes the playable audio of an existing Sound while
preserving that Sound's identity.

**Audio draft**:
A Sound and its prepared audio assembled for review before being added to the Sound catalog.
